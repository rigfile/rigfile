## Reporting a rig

Signed-in users can report any rig they can see, using the **Report this rig** link on its page. Choose a reason (malware, leaked secret, illegal content, abuse, impersonation, other) and describe the problem. To report a security problem in the registry itself, or something you cannot report through the form, write to bytebuilderslab@gmail.com.

## What we do with a report

1. An administrator reads it, normally within 5 business days.
2. If the rig breaks the rules, we **remove** the affected version(s), or the whole rig for serious cases, and may suspend the publisher. A removed version is unavailable to everyone but administrators; we keep the file and the record for the audit trail and for legal obligations.
3. For a leaked secret, we remove the version so it stops being served, but **you must still revoke the secret**: anyone who already downloaded the rig has a copy.
4. The report is closed as *actioned* or *dismissed*. Every step is written to an audit log.

## Yanking and removal are different

*Yank* is done by the publisher: the version is hidden from new resolutions but still downloadable by exact version, so existing lockfiles keep working; people who pull it see a warning. *Removal* is done by us: the version is unavailable.

## Appeals and legal notices

If your rig was removed or your account suspended and you think that was a mistake, write to bytebuilderslab@gmail.com with the rig name and your reasoning. For copyright or other legal notices, write to bytebuilderslab@gmail.com and include what is needed for the notice to be valid where you live.
