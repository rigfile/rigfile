New collection

A curated, ordered list of rigs — yours, other people's, or a mix — grouped for discovery (like "starter rigs" or "rigs for data work"). Public by default, or keep it to yourself.

Same thing from the CLI:

rigfile collection create starter-rigs --title "Starter rigs"
rigfile collection add starter-rigs owner/name
rigfile collection show {{.Data.Login}}/starter-rigs
New organisation

A shared namespace several people publish under (like a GitHub org), instead of everyone's individual account — rigs are named org/rig. Every member can publish and yank; owners and admins manage members and decide visibility.

You become its owner. Publish under it as name/rig.

Same thing from the CLI:

rigfile org create acme --title "Acme"
rigfile org add acme their-login --role member
rigfile publish my-rig --to-registry --name acme/my-rig
